  \item \label{trace:overrides-MyOuter-MyInner}Compute $\overrides((\text{``MyOuter''}, \text{``MyInner''}))$, the paths sharing the identity of $(\text{``MyOuter''}, \text{``MyInner''})$.     \begin{aligned}
      &\overrides((\text{``HasMultipleOuters''}, \text{``outer''})) ={} \\
      &\quad \left\{\begin{aligned}

  \]
  \item \label{trace:bases-Has-outer}Compute $\bases((\text{``HasMultipleOuters''}, \text{``outer''}))$. It resolves the references carried by the overrides of $(\text{``HasMultipleOuters''}, \text{``outer''})$ at the enclosing scope, and collects their targets:

    \end{aligned}

    \end{aligned}
